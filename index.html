Answers to Your Biggest Questions

We know choosing a coaching institute is a major decision. Here's everything parents and students ask us most.

Our classroom strength is capped at 40 students per batch. We run three parallel batches for each course — Regular (6 am–10 am), Afternoon (1 pm–5 pm), and Weekend (Saturday–Sunday). Every student is placed in a batch after a diagnostic test, ensuring similar academic levels in the same class. Teaching pace is reviewed monthly and adjusted based on batch performance data. Students who are ahead of pace can join our advanced accelerator track at no extra charge.
Not at all — in fact, Class X is the ideal time. Students who join our Foundation Programme in Class X build conceptual depth 18 months before their peers. This means that by the time they reach Class XI JEE/NEET content, they're not learning from scratch — they're refining. Our Foundation-to-JEE transition students consistently outperform those who join fresh in Class XI by an average of 12 percentile points.
Our Early Alert System (EAS) flags any student who scores below 60% in two consecutive chapter tests. The student's academic mentor immediately schedules a 1-on-1 session to identify the root cause — whether it's a conceptual gap, time management issue, or personal stress. A customised 30-day recovery plan is then created and tracked weekly. Parents are informed at every step. We've brought students from 45% to 85% in 60 days using this system — it's not rare at Pravin Tutorials, it's our standard.
Yes — we run the PT Merit Scholarship Test (PTMST) every month. Students scoring above 80% receive 25% fee waiver, above 90% receive 50% waiver, and above 95% receive 100% fee waiver (full scholarship). Scholarships are renewable annually based on internal exam performance. Additionally, students from economically weaker sections (EWS) can apply for our Special Assistance Fund — we never let financial constraints stop a deserving student.
Every lecture at Pravin Tutorials is recorded and uploaded to our app within 4 hours of the class ending. Students can watch it at home at their own pace, with speed control and chapter markers. Additionally, makeup doubt-clearing sessions run every Saturday from 8 am to 2 pm. If a student misses more than 3 consecutive classes, the academic mentor calls the parents directly to understand the situation and arrange support.
Pravin Tutorials' main campus is at Kothrud, Pune — easily accessible from Deccan, Shivajinagar, Warje, Bavdhan, and Aundh. Our second branch is at Wakad. We have a dedicated transport service with GPS-tracked vans from Pashan, Baner, Pimple Saudagar, Khadakwasla, and Sinhagad Road. Route maps and pickup timings are available at our front desk or on the app.
